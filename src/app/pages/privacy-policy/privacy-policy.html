2. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted on a web server of Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany. When you open the website, the server automatically stores information that your browser sends: IP address, date and time of the request, the page requested, the referring page, browser type and operating system. This data is needed to deliver the website securely and to detect errors and attacks (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). It is deleted after seven days at the latest, unless it is needed to clarify a security incident.

3. Contact form

When you send a message through the contact form, I process your name, your email address and your message to answer your request. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR if your request concerns a possible cooperation, otherwise my legitimate interest in answering requests (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). I delete the data as soon as your request is settled and no legal retention periods apply.

The message is stored in a database on the server named above. To prevent abuse, the server also keeps a one-way hash of your IP address, which limits the number of messages per sender. Without a secret key the hash cannot be traced back to your address, and it is deleted after one hour (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

4. No cookies, no tracking

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or tracking tools. The fonts are stored on this server, so no connection to external font services is made.

5. Links to GitHub and LinkedIn

The icons for GitHub and LinkedIn are plain links. Data is only transferred to these services when you click a link; from then on, the privacy policy of the respective provider applies.
